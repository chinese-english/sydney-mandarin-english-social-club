About us

A weekly speaking club built around preparation, repetition, and real conversation.

We are a social club because people come to socialise through language, not sit through lectures. The structure exists to get people speaking quickly, make beginners comfortable, and turn practice into something real from the first minute.

How it works

Structured from the start, social all the way through.

01

Prepare first

English speakers prepare on the Chinese page. Chinese speakers prepare on the English page.

02

Speak with strangers

You use those prepared lines with people you do not already know, which is where real speaking confidence starts to form.

03

Rotate and repeat

You say the same ideas several times to different people, which improves fluency, clarity, and comfort.

04

Keep socialising

After the structured part, the meetup continues as real conversation over food, coffee, or a walk around the city.

Why this works

Pronunciation, confidence, and spontaneity are built in live interaction.

Pronunciation improves in public

You say something, someone misunderstands you, you try again, and over time you become easier for real people to understand.

Structure reduces fear

The prep pages remove the blank-page feeling. You arrive with something ready, so speaking begins faster and with less hesitation.

Strangers create real growth

It is different when you cannot rely on familiarity. That is where your speaking becomes clearer, more flexible, and more real.
