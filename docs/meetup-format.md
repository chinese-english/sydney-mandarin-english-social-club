# Meetup Format

This document captures the repeatable structure for Sydney Mandarin and English Social Club meetups.

## Goal

Create a relaxed in-person environment where people can practice Mandarin and English consistently while also enjoying social time around Sydney.

## Standard Flow

### 1. Arrival And Warm-Up

- Short self-introductions
- Easy opening questions
- Light conversation to reduce pressure

Suggested prompts:

- What's your name?
- Where are you from?
- Why are you learning Mandarin or English?
- What have you been doing this week?

### 2. Guided Practice

- Share a small set of prepared sentences or prompts
- Pair people up or form small groups
- Switch between Mandarin and English
- Keep topics simple, practical, and repeatable

Suggested topic categories:

- Self-introduction
- Hobbies
- Travel
- Work or study
- Food
- Culture
- Weekend plans
- Sydney life

### 3. Social Time

- Dinner
- Takeaway
- Coffee
- Walking and talking
- Parks, libraries, museums, or other city venues

The final section should feel unforced and conversational. The guided section builds momentum; the social section turns practice into natural interaction.

## Core Principles

- Beginner-friendly
- No pressure to be perfect
- Consistency over intensity
- Balanced language exchange
- Social connection matters

## Venue Style

Meetups should stay practical, accessible, and easy to join after work or study hours. Evening scheduling supports that format well.
