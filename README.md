# Sydney Mandarin and English Social Club

This repository stores the core description and operating notes for the Sydney Mandarin and English Social Club.

## Website

The repository includes a static website:

- `index.html`: attendee-focused homepage
- `chinese.html`: Chinese preparation page for English speakers learning Chinese
- `english.html`: interactive English preparation page
- `sentence-prep.js`: shared sentence selection, persistence, and rendering logic
- `styles.css`: shared styling

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the main landing page.

## Overview

Sydney Mandarin and English Social Club is an in-person language exchange group for people who want to practice Mandarin Chinese and English in a relaxed, social, and supportive environment.

Whether someone is a beginner or fluent, the goal is simple:

- Speak more
- Meet people
- Improve naturally

## What The Club Is

The club meets in the evenings and combines language practice with social activities. Meetups can happen over dinner or takeaway, or in places like parks, libraries, museums, and other interesting spots around Sydney.

The idea is to practice language while experiencing the city together.

## Who It Is For

- English speakers learning Mandarin
- Mandarin speakers learning English
- Anyone interested in language and cultural exchange

Fluency is not required. Participants only need to be willing to try.

## Meetup Structure

Each meetup follows a simple structure so people are never stuck not knowing what to say.

### 1. Introductions And Warm-Up

Short introductions and light conversation to help everyone settle in.

### 2. Guided Speaking Practice

Participants receive a small set of prepared sentences or prompts before or at the meetup. Topics change each time and may include:

- Introducing yourself
- Hobbies
- Travel
- Work
- Food
- Culture

People practice in pairs or small groups and switch between Mandarin and English.

### 3. Relaxed Social Time

After guided practice, the meetup shifts into a more natural social setting such as dinner, coffee, or walking and talking.

This is where conversations become more free and organic.

## Atmosphere

The group is designed to feel:

- Friendly
- Supportive
- Low-pressure
- Social
- Consistent

There is no pressure to be perfect. The goal is to build confidence through regular practice.

## Why Join

By joining, participants can:

- Get regular speaking practice
- Meet new people
- Build consistency
- Improve confidence
- Make language learning social and enjoyable
