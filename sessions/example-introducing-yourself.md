# Example Session: Introducing Yourself

## Details

The focus of this session is simple: introducing yourself in both Mandarin and English.

You do not need to be fluent. You can read from your phone or from printed phrases. The goal is to practise speaking out loud and get comfortable.

## Before The Session

To make the session more productive, attendees should prepare a short script before arrival.

Use the suggested phrases below and write what you are going to say in the language you are learning.

Post your prepared script in Slack before the session begins:

<https://join.slack.com/t/english-mandarin/shared_invite/zt-3wuw68g1c-tjv8s2~DBCpo8RYU_V0htQ>

- If you are learning Mandarin, write your introduction in Mandarin.
- If you are learning English, write your introduction in English.

It does not need to be perfect. The purpose is to come prepared, practise speaking, and get feedback from others.

## How It Works

We start with a short welcome and quick explanation.

Then everyone practises a simple self-introduction using their prepared script. Each person introduces themselves multiple times to different people in small groups or pairs.

Participants speak in the language they are learning, not their native language.

Partners rotate every 10 to 15 minutes so everyone gets multiple chances to practise.

After the structured part, the session moves into more relaxed conversation over food or drinks.

This is a friendly and supportive environment. It is completely okay to make mistakes or read from your phone.

## Suggested Phrases

### English

- Hello, my name is ___
- I am from ___
- I live in ___
- I work as a ___ / I study ___
- I am learning Mandarin because ___
- My hobbies are ___
- Nice to meet you

### Mandarin Simplified Chinese + Pinyin

- 你好，我叫 ___
  `Nǐ hǎo, wǒ jiào ___`
- 我来自 ___
  `Wǒ láizì ___`
- 我住在 ___
  `Wǒ zhù zài ___`
- 我是做 ___ 的 / 我是学生
  `Wǒ shì zuò ___ de / Wǒ shì xuéshēng`
- 我学习中文因为 ___
  `Wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén yīnwèi ___`
- 我的爱好是 ___
  `Wǒ de àihào shì ___`
- 很高兴认识你
  `Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ`
