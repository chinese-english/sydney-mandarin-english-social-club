Keep these parts intact

  • Prepared prompts before arrival
  • One clear topic per session
  • Rotation every 10 to 15 minutes
  • Speaking in the target language
  • Social time after the structured section

Method

Use the template. Keep the tone. Adapt the city.

Choose the simplest venue

Evening cafes, food courts, libraries, parks, museums, and walking routes all work. Convenience matters more than novelty.

Publish the topic early

Prepared attendees create a stronger room. Share the topic and prompt sheet before the session, not at the door.

Facilitate rotation deliberately

Do not let the room freeze into fixed cliques. Rotation is part of the method, not an optional extra.

Protect the social finish

The organised section creates momentum. The informal section is where the chapter becomes worth returning to.
