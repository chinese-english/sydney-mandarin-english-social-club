Current meetup

Every Wednesday night, 7-8pm, in and around central Sydney. Locations may vary.

How it helps

Preparation reduces hesitation. When you already have your lines, it is much easier to start speaking with strangers.

How it saves

Your details and selections stay on this device so you can come back, edit them, and reuse them next time.

How translation works here

  • Fixed sentence parts are already prepared in English, Mandarin, and pinyin
  • For your personal details, you can add English and Mandarin versions
  • If you add pinyin for your personal details, it appears in the final script too
  • If you leave fields blank, the builder leaves a space for you to complete later
